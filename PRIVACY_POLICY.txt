PRIVACY POLICY — CHRIS FAMOUS UPLOADER
=============================================
Last updated: [FILL IN DATE WHEN YOU PUBLISH THIS]

This Privacy Policy describes how Chris Famous Uploader ("the app," "this
tool") handles data. This is a personal-use application built and operated
by a single individual (Chris Haggerty, operating the "Chris Famous"
content channel) for the sole purpose of publishing my own original video
content to my own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

WHO THIS APPLIES TO
This app is not available to the public and is not offered as a service
to any third party. It is used exclusively by its owner/operator to
manage their own personal content accounts.

WHAT DATA THIS APP ACCESSES
- OAuth authentication tokens issued by TikTok and YouTube, used solely
  to authorize this app to publish video content to the owner's own
  connected accounts.
- Video files, titles, descriptions, and tags created by the owner for
  their own content, generated locally to prepare content for publishing.
- Basic channel/account analytics (such as subscriber counts, view
  counts, and posting-time recommendations) retrieved from the owner's
  own connected accounts, used only to inform the owner's own content and
  scheduling decisions.

WHAT THIS APP DOES NOT DO
- This app does not collect, access, or store data belonging to any
  TikTok or YouTube user other than its owner.
- This app does not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.
- This app does not serve advertising or use collected data for
  advertising purposes.
- This app does not offer login, sign-up, or account access to any
  outside user.

DATA STORAGE
Authentication tokens and content files are stored locally on the
owner's own personal computer and are not transmitted to any third-party
server beyond the minimum necessary API calls to TikTok and YouTube
required to publish content and retrieve the owner's own account data.

DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
The owner may revoke this app's access to their TikTok or YouTube account
at any time through TikTok's or YouTube's own account settings, which
immediately invalidates all stored authentication tokens.
